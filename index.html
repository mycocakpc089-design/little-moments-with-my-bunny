For my bunny ♡

Bunny, you may not be my first love, but I want you to be my last. I’ve been through pain in the past, and I know love isn’t always easy, but I don’t want my past to stop me from loving you wholeheartedly. I don’t know what the future holds for us, and I can’t promise that everything will always be perfect. But right now, you’re the person I want to experience life with. I want to do so many things with you, watch the moon and rivers together, share my random thoughts, make you laugh, make you blush, and be there for you on both your good and bad days. I want you to feel safe, comfortable, understood, and loved when you’re with me. I don’t just want to know your beautiful side; I want to understand the parts of you that you might be afraid to show, too. I don’t care about being better than anyone else. I just want to be someone who loves you genuinely and makes you happy in ways you’ll always remember. Even if I can’t predict our future, I don’t want to waste the beautiful present we have together. If you’re going to be my last love, I want you to know that I’ll never regret choosing to love you. I’ll cherish the time we share, the little moments, and every memory we create. And if you ever look back on us one day, I hope you smile and remember that someone once loved you with all his heart. ❤️

Always, with sincerity ♡
For my bunny ♡ ☾
